About this project

MindTrack analyses sentences and emotional shifts across your journal using a word-frequency model, an expanded vocabulary, contextual phrases, slang and emoji recognition. It recognises 19 moods and identifies earlier feelings and the current mood. Predictions may misunderstand sarcasm or complex context. Your self-selected mood always takes priority.

Educational tool only, not a diagnosis. If you are struggling, consider talking to a trusted adult, school counsellor or qualified professional.
