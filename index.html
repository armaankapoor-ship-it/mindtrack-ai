About this project

MindTrack analyses sentences and emotional shifts across your journal using a word-frequency model, an expanded vocabulary, contextual phrases, slang and emoji recognition. It recognises 26 primary moods and supports 31 separate intensity ratings. The emotional analytics view maps narrative context, performance comparisons, future intentions, and mixed feelings to editable dimension scores. Predictions may misunderstand sarcasm or complex context. Your self-selected mood always takes priority.

Educational tool only, not a diagnosis. If you are struggling, consider talking to a trusted adult, school counsellor or qualified professional.

How these scores work

MindTrack estimates pleasantness (−5 to +5), activation, motivation, perceived control, and the intensity of each emotion (0 to 10). These are dimensionless project scores, inspired by Russell’s circumplex model and the pleasure, arousal, and dominance dimensions of the Self-Assessment Manikin.

The AI uses context and emotional evidence to suggest ratings. Review and adjust them before saving. Intensity is a suggested strength, while AI confidence indicates how clear the textual evidence is. Neither is a clinical measurement.
